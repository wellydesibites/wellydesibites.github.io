How much notice do I need for a custom birthday cake?

One week’s notice is recommended for a custom cake, especially where a particular design, flavour or dietary requirement is important. This ensures we have the capacity and specific ingredients to make your celebration special.

In your first message, try to include your celebration date, guest count, any design inspiration images, and your preferred flavour. We will always try to help with last-minute enquiries where possible, so don't hesitate to ask!

Eggless, vegan and gluten-free cakes: what should I ask for?

Every WellyDesiBites cake is 100% eggless. We specialise in creating moisture and flavour without eggs, so you never have to compromise on taste.

Vegan and gluten-free options are available on request. Please share your dietary requirements when you first enquire. This lets us discuss the most suitable flavour combinations and design techniques for your specific needs.

Choosing a cake flavour and size for your celebration

Choosing the right size depends on two main factors: the number of guests and whether the cake is the primary dessert or part of a larger sweets table. For milestone birthdays, you might want a taller or multi-tiered cake to make a statement.

Our flavour menu ranges from classic fruit combinations to rich Desi-inspired signatures like Rabri or Gulab Jamun fusion. Every cake we make is uniquely quoted based on its size, design complexity, and chosen flavour profile.
