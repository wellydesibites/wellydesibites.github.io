Frequently asked questions

Do you make only eggless cakes?

Yes—WellyDesiBites specialises in 100% eggless cakes, cupcakes and pastries.

Can I request vegan or gluten-free options?

Yes. Tell us your dietary requirements when you message so we can discuss the right option for you.

How much notice do you need?

One week’s notice is recommended. We will always try to help with last-minute enquiries where possible.

Do you deliver in Wellington?

Yes, delivery is available within the Wellington region for an additional charge.
